# Changelog

## 1.0.5 – October 21, 2026

* fixed: scrolling over an embed that uses the mouse wheel itself, such as a Google Map zooming with Cmd/Ctrl + scroll or a 3D viewer, no longer scrolls the page underneath.

## 1.0.4 – October 13, 2026

* fixed: links to panels inside Arts Horizontal Scroll sections keep working after that plugin's update.

## 1.0.3 – October 8, 2026

* fixed: anchor links that tabs, menus or other scripts have already handled are no longer scrolled a second time.
* fixed: links to panels inside Arts Horizontal Scroll sections now scroll to the right panel.

## 1.0.2 – September 20, 2026

* improved: updated the Lenis smooth-scrolling engine.

## 1.0.1 – August 18, 2026

* improved: confirmed compatibility with WordPress 7.1.

## 1.0.0 – August 17, 2026

Initial release.
